About Us

A community built around comfort and care.

Lilic's Apartment started with a simple idea: renting a home shouldn't feel transactional. Here's our story.

Our story

Lilic's Apartment began as a single renovated building with a handful of units and a promise to treat every resident like a neighbor, not a tenant number. Over the years we've grown, but that promise hasn't changed.

Today, our property features a mix of studios, one-bedrooms, and two-bedroom units, each renovated with an eye for comfort, natural light, and quality finishes — all backed by a management team that actually answers the phone.
